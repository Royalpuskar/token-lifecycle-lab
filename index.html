    Why does reuse revoke the whole family?

    Once R1 is exchanged for R2, a second use of R1 may mean the original token was copied. This model denies all subsequent family operations. It deliberately models online revocation checks: a real stateless access token might remain usable until it expires unless the service checks revocation.

    No login, JWT signing, storage, or authentication service is implemented here. Reset or reload clears the experiment.
